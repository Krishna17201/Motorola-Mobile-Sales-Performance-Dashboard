# 📱 Motorola Mobile Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze mobile sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product trends, and payment preferences. This project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through KPI tracking, data visualization, and interactive filtering.

**Project Type:** Data Analytics | Business Intelligence | Sales Performance Analysis

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* Microsoft Power BI Desktop – Dashboard development and interactive reporting
* Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
* DAX – KPI calculations and analytical measures
* Microsoft Excel – Dataset handling and exploration
* Data Visualization – KPI Cards, Bar Charts, Line Charts, Maps, and Slicers

## 🎯 Business Problem

Mobile sales businesses generate large volumes of transactional data across different products, brands, cities, payment methods, and customer interactions.

Without a consolidated reporting system, stakeholders may find it difficult to:

* Monitor overall sales performance and revenue trends.
* Identify high-performing mobile brands and models.
* Understand geographical sales distribution.
* Evaluate customer purchasing and payment preferences.
* Track customer ratings and satisfaction patterns.
* Identify sales fluctuations across different time periods.

The challenge is to transform raw transactional data into an interactive reporting solution that supports informed business analysis.

## 🎯 Project Goal

To develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that consolidates mobile sales data into meaningful KPIs and visual insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor business performance, explore sales patterns, and identify potential growth opportunities.

**Key Objectives:**

* Track total sales, quantity sold, transactions, and average selling price.
* Compare sales performance across mobile brands and models.
* Analyze geographical sales distribution.
* Examine monthly sales trends and customer purchasing behaviour.
* Understand payment method preferences and customer ratings.
* Enable dynamic analysis through interactive slicers and filters.

## 📊 Key Visuals & Why I Used Them

**1. KPI Cards**

* Display key metrics such as total sales, quantity sold, transactions, and average price.
* Provide an immediate overview of business performance.

**2. Line Chart**

* Analyze monthly sales trends and fluctuations.
* Help identify changes in sales performance over time.

**3. Bar and Column Charts**

* Compare sales across mobile brands and models.
* Highlight product-level performance differences.

**4. Geographic Map**

* Visualize city-wise sales distribution.
* Help explore regional sales contribution.

**5. Payment Method Analysis**

* Examine customer payment preferences.
* Provide insights into purchasing behaviour.

**6. Customer Ratings Distribution**

* Understand customer feedback patterns.
* Support analysis of customer satisfaction.

**7. Interactive Slicers**

* Allow users to filter data dynamically.
* Enable focused analysis across different categories and dimensions.

## 💡 Business Impact

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of mobile sales performance and supports data-driven business analysis.

* **Sales Monitoring:** Track key performance indicators through a centralized dashboard.
* **Product Performance:** Identify brands and models contributing to overall sales.
* **Regional Analysis:** Explore geographical differences in sales performance.
* **Customer Understanding:** Examine payment preferences and customer ratings.
* **Performance Tracking:** Monitor trends and fluctuations across time periods.
* **Decision Support:** Help stakeholders investigate sales patterns and formulate data-informed business strategies.

*Note: This is an educational dashboard project. Business impact refers to potential analytical applications rather than measured operational improvements.*

## 🔍 Key Insights & Analytical Questions

The dashboard enables exploration of the following business questions:

* Which mobile brands and models contribute most to sales?
* How does sales performance change across different months?
* Which cities demonstrate higher sales contribution?
* Which payment methods are most commonly used?
* How are customer ratings distributed?
* How do sales quantity, transaction volume, and average selling price vary across selected categories?

**Analytical Value:** By combining KPI monitoring, trend analysis, product comparisons, geographical visualization, and customer behaviour analysis, the dashboard translates transactional data into meaningful business intelligence.
